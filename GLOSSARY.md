# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners and teams. This glossary is the shared language for buying courses and asking for money back.

## Language

### Buying

**Purchase**:
One payment by a buyer for a Course, either for themselves or for team seats. The buyer is not always the person who learns.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a Course. It is separate from any Purchase: a learner who redeems a team seat has an Enrollment but no Purchase of their own.

### Getting money back

**Refund Request**:
A buyer's request, with a reason, to get money back for a Purchase. The team reviews each one by hand.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Pending**:
A Refund Request the team has not reviewed yet. Every Refund Request starts here.
_Avoid_: Requested, Open

**Approved**:
A Refund Request the team accepted, which results in a Refund.

**Declined**:
A Refund Request the team turned down. No money is sent back.

**Refund**:
The money sent back to the buyer when a Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days from a Purchase during which its buyer may raise a Refund Request.
